Host a game

You play as Mario on your own save; players who join add to it. When your game starts, its room code is copied for you to send to friends, and shows here, under Play and on the game's pause screen. No port forwarding needed. (Friends joining with your address instead need UDP port 24002 forwarded to this computer.)

Join a game

The host presses Host Game first and sends you their room code. You join as Luigi, Wario, Metal Mario or a Mario in his own colours, and go straight to wherever the host is.

    Checking your display…

    Sets everything under Quality below. Change any of them and the preset becomes Custom.

    Still seeing frame drops? Try a lower screen resolution: under Display, set Full screen type to Exclusive and choose a lower Exclusive resolution.

    Quality

    FSR 1 (AMD) and NIS (NVIDIA Image Scaling) render below your screen's resolution and upscale with clean edges: Quality at 67%, Balanced 59%, Performance 50%, Ultra Performance 33%, Native at full size. They set the resolution themselves, so Picture sharpness is set aside while one is on. Sharpening sets how crisp they are. Both work on any graphics card.

    HD visuals

    Checking for HD textures…

    Custom texturesPut texture packs made for Dolphin here: they replace the HD textures where both change the same texture, and work with HD textures off too.

    Custom cutscenesPut your own Sunshine movies here, such as ones from the Switch, as .thp files named like the game's (openingA.thp): they play instead of the disc's or the HD cutscenes, with HD cutscenes on or off.

    GameCube adapter

    On SteamOS or a Steam Deck?

    Steam Input can translate your controller into its own layout before the game sees it. That can make buttons do two things, or make the controller buttons you set here not apply. If that happens, turn Steam Input off for SMS Launcher: in Steam, open its Properties → Controller and choose Disable Steam Input. Then restart the launcher.

    Playing in Desktop Mode with the launcher added to Steam as a non-Steam game is where this usually shows up.
