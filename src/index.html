Quality

FSR 1 (AMD) and NIS (NVIDIA Image Scaling) render below your screen's resolution and upscale with clean edges: Quality at 67%, Balanced 59%, Performance 50%, Ultra Performance 33%, Native at full size. They set the resolution themselves, so Picture sharpness is set aside while one is on. Sharpening sets how crisp they are. Both work on any graphics card.

GameCube adapter

On SteamOS or a Steam Deck?

Steam Input can translate your controller into its own layout before the game sees it. That can make buttons do two things, or make the controller buttons you set here not apply. If that happens, turn Steam Input off for SMS Launcher: in Steam, open its Properties → Controller and choose Disable Steam Input. Then restart the launcher.

Playing in Desktop Mode with the launcher added to Steam as a non-Steam game is where this usually shows up.

Host a game

You play as Mario on your own save; players who join add to it. When your game starts, its room code is copied for you to send to friends, and shows under the play button and on the game's pause screen. No port forwarding needed. (Friends joining with your address instead need UDP port 24002 forwarded to this computer.)

Join a game

The host presses Host Game first and sends you their room code. You join as Luigi, Peach or Metal Mario, and go straight to wherever the host is.

Recent

Shines, blue coins, red coins, coins and lives are shared, and everyone sees the host's enemies and each other's water. Only the host can take everyone to another area. A Shine collected by anyone takes everyone back out together. Out of lives, you watch the others until everyone is out, then the level starts again. Save states are off while playing together.
